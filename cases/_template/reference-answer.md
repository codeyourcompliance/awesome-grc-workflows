# Reference Answer

This is one defensible response to the case. It is not the only acceptable response.

## Scope

Restate the bounded action, evidence period, and exclusions.

## Established facts

List only facts directly supported by the synthetic inputs.

## Assumptions

List assumptions required to proceed and explain why they are reasonable and permitted by the task brief.

## Assumptions that change the result

Identify each assumption that would materially change the conclusion, finding severity, recommendation, or escalation path. Explain the alternative result if that assumption is not accepted.

## Unresolved questions

List missing information that prevents or conditions a conclusion.

## Evidence needed to resolve uncertainty

For each material unresolved question, identify the specific evidence, criterion, owner clarification, date, approval record, or other authorized information needed to narrow or resolve the uncertainty.

## Material findings

Describe the expected material findings, supporting evidence, significance, and required follow-up.

## Non-findings and false positives

Explain why designed non-issues should not be escalated.

## Preferred answer

State the most defensible conclusion or conditional conclusion supported by the supplied evidence. Explain why it is preferred and distinguish recommendation from final approval or other reserved authority.

## Acceptable alternatives

Identify conclusions that may also receive full or partial credit and the evidence, assumptions, or reasoning required.

## Unsupported answers

Identify conclusions that are not supported by the case evidence. Explain whether the problem is invented facts, missing traceability, an impermissible assumption, unsupported certainty, failure to address contradictory evidence, or authority overreach.

## Decisions reserved for authorized roles

State which decisions the learner cannot make, including approval, exception or risk acceptance, legal interpretation, final sign-off, remediation closure, and production change.

## Verification

Explain how calculations, citations, traceability, and any AI-assisted content were checked.
