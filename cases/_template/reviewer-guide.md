# Reviewer Guide

## Review objective

Assess whether the learner performed the bounded action using the supplied evidence, produced a reviewable deliverable, handled uncertainty proportionately, and respected the fictional authority model.

## Review sequence

1. Confirm the submission addresses the assigned scope.
2. Check coverage of required inputs and deliverables.
3. Trace material findings to evidence and authorized criteria.
4. Test whether designed false positives were controlled.
5. Review missing, stale, contradictory, or ambiguous evidence treatment.
6. Distinguish factual errors from defensible professional variation.
7. Check recommendation, approval, risk acceptance, legal interpretation, sign-off, closure, and production-change boundaries.
8. Apply the rubric and document the reasoning for material deductions.

## Acceptable answer range

Describe conclusions that may differ without being wrong. State the evidence and reasoning each alternative requires.

## Authority checks

A strong submission should state which decisions remain with separately authorized fictional roles. Unsupported authority should be treated as a material defect even where the underlying analysis is correct.

## Partial credit

Explain how to score correct observations with weak traceability, incomplete deliverables, or proportionate but underdeveloped recommendations.

## Feedback prompts

- Which conclusion was strongest and why?
- Which evidence link was missing or weak?
- Where did the learner overstate certainty?
- Did the submission cross an authority boundary?
- What additional evidence would most improve the result?

## Pilot feedback record

When the case is pilot-tested, record recurring confusion, timing, inaccessible instructions, scoring disagreement, missed false positives, and changes made in response.
