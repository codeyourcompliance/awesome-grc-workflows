# Scoring Rubric

## Scoring model

Total: 100 points.

Adapt the weights to the bounded action. Explain every material departure from this baseline.

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Scope discipline | 10 | Performs the assigned action and excludes adjacent work or unauthorized decisions. |
| Evidence coverage and traceability | 20 | Links material conclusions to the correct inputs, criteria, versions, and dates. |
| Material issue detection | 20 | Identifies the designed material issues and explains their significance. |
| False-positive control | 10 | Correctly avoids treating plausible non-issues as findings. |
| Uncertainty and contradiction handling | 15 | Identifies missing, stale, inconsistent, or ambiguous information and responds proportionately. |
| Deliverable quality | 10 | Produces a complete, usable, professionally structured work product. |
| Authority-boundary discipline | 10 | Separates recommendation from approval, risk acceptance, legal determination, sign-off, closure, and production change. |
| Verification and communication | 5 | Checks calculations, citations, traceability, and AI-assisted output; communicates clearly. |

## Performance anchors

| Score | Interpretation |
| ---: | --- |
| 90–100 | Strong professional submission with complete traceability, proportionate judgment, and no material authority overreach. |
| 75–89 | Defensible submission with limited omissions or minor reasoning defects. |
| 60–74 | Partially usable but contains material gaps, weak traceability, or inconsistent judgment. |
| Below 60 | Not review-ready; misses material issues, invents support, or fails core scope and authority requirements. |

## Critical errors

A reviewer may cap or fail a submission that:

- invents facts, evidence, criteria, or citations;
- treats an unsupported inference as a case fact;
- grants itself final approval, risk acceptance, legal authority, sign-off, closure authority, or production authority;
- ignores a material unresolved deficiency;
- exposes confidential or personal information;
- changes production state where the case is read-only;
- claims a named employer uses the synthetic case process;
- relies on unverified AI output for a material conclusion.

## Case-specific scoring notes

List:

- mandatory findings;
- acceptable alternative findings;
- designed false positives;
- intentional ambiguities;
- evidence that would justify different conclusions;
- any score cap or automatic-fail condition.
