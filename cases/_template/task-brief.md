# Task Brief

## Your role

State the fictional learner role and its limited authority.

## Assignment

Describe one bounded work action using an operational verb, defined object, comparison basis, and decision target.

## Materials provided

List every file or evidence class in `inputs/`.

## Required tools

List the tools required to complete the assignment, accepted equivalents, and any product or version constraints. If no specialized tool is required, state that explicitly.

Keep optional tools and implementation choices separate. The learner must not be penalized for using an accepted equivalent unless the case genuinely depends on a specific product or version.

## Required deliverables

| Deliverable | Format | Required contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary work product | Markdown, spreadsheet, or other stated format | Scope, evidence reviewed, analysis, findings, unresolved questions, and recommendation |

## Required distinctions

The submission must separate:

- case facts;
- assumptions;
- unsupported or missing information;
- findings;
- recommendations;
- decisions reserved for an authorized role.

## Assumption rules

### Permitted assumptions

List only the limited assumptions the learner may make to perform the bounded action. Each permitted assumption must be identified in the submission and must not replace material evidence or authority.

### Prohibited assumptions

The learner must not invent or assume:

- approvals, exceptions, risk acceptance, legal conclusions, sign-off, closure, or production authority;
- criteria, dates, evidence, system state, ownership, scope, or materiality not supplied by the case;
- that missing evidence is satisfactory evidence;
- that an inferred or case-designed output was required by a real employer;
- that a named organization uses the fictional process.

### When evidence is insufficient

The learner should:

1. state the unresolved question or deficiency;
2. identify the additional evidence or authorized clarification needed;
3. explain how the uncertainty affects the analysis;
4. use a conditional recommendation or escalation rather than forcing a final conclusion.

## Included scope

- State the actions the learner should perform.

## Excluded scope

The learner must not assume authority to:

- approve final closure or an exception;
- accept residual risk;
- make legal or regulatory determinations;
- execute remediation;
- change production state;
- override unresolved deficiencies;
- sign off a conclusion unless the fictional scenario expressly grants that authority.

## Submission requirements

State naming, format, length, citation, traceability, and calculation requirements.

## Use of AI or automation

AI or automation may be used only within the stated case rules. The learner remains responsible for verifying facts, calculations, citations, traceability, and authority boundaries.

## Completion condition

Define what must be submitted for the case to be reviewable. Do not require the learner to force a final decision where evidence is insufficient.
