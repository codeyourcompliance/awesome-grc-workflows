# Remediation Evidence Closure Review

> This case uses a fictional organization and synthetic or safely constructed data. It does not represent any named employer’s internal process, systems, controls, authority structure, or records.

## Bounded work action

Review submitted remediation evidence and issue-closure artifacts against documented closure requirements before issue closure.

## Learning objective

Assess whether a submitted closure package is complete, current, traceable, internally consistent, and aligned with documented requirements, then communicate a defensible closure-readiness recommendation without crossing approval or risk-acceptance boundaries.

## Decision target

Determine whether the submitted documentary evidence is sufficient to support the next authorized closure decision.

## Required tools

- Spreadsheet editor capable of opening CSV files.
- Document editor capable of producing Markdown or an equivalent reviewable document.

Accepted equivalents are permitted. No vendor-specific product or version is required.

## Included scope

- Compare submitted artifacts with documented closure requirements.
- Assess relevance, completeness, freshness, reliability, consistency, and traceability.
- Identify missing, stale, contradictory, duplicated, or unsupported materials.
- Prepare a closure-readiness recommendation and targeted evidence requests.

## Excluded scope

- Remediation execution or production change.
- Control retesting beyond review of the supplied test artifact.
- Determining root-cause resolution or sustained remediation effectiveness.
- Final issue closure, exception approval, residual-risk acceptance, legal interpretation, or final sign-off.

## Authority boundary

The learner may analyze evidence, record deficiencies, and recommend whether the package is ready for authorized closure consideration. The learner may not close the issue, approve an exception, accept risk, make a legal determination, execute remediation, or change production state.

## Required deliverables

1. Evidence-to-requirement review workpaper.
2. Evidence deficiency register.
3. Closure-readiness memo.

- [The Institute of Internal Auditors — Complete Global Internal Audit Standards](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/standards/complete-global-internal-audit-standards/). Principle 15 and Standard 15.2 provide general professional context for confirming implementation of recommendations or action plans through an established follow-up methodology. The resource does not define this fictional organization’s closure requirements, materiality, evidence sufficiency, or approval authority.

## Known limitations

This case trains documentary closure-readiness review. It does not teach a complete issue-management program, remediation-effectiveness validation, legal interpretation, risk acceptance, or production change control.
