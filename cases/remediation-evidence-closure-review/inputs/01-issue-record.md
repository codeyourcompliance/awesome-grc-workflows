# Issue Record CQ-247

- Title: Incomplete approval evidence for FleetOps privileged access
- Opened: 2026-04-08
- Issue owner: Omar Reyes
- Remediation owner: Maya Chen
- Severity: Moderate
- Status: Pending closure review
- Affected process: FleetOps privileged access provisioning
- Review population: Privileged access requests created from 2026-05-21 through 2026-06-15

## Condition

Seven of twenty-five privileged access requests reviewed during the original assessment lacked retained evidence of both manager and FleetOps application-owner approval.

## Cause

The request workflow permitted fulfillment before both approval records were attached to the ticket.

## Risk statement

Privileged access could be granted without complete authorization evidence, reducing accountability and increasing the risk of inappropriate access.

## Approved remediation objective

Configure the workflow to require both approvals before fulfillment, remove one orphan privileged account, update the procedure, and demonstrate compliant operation through the documented closure requirements.

## Authority note

The Evidence Review Analyst may recommend readiness. The Issue Closure Committee decides closure. Risk acceptance, if proposed, requires the Risk Acceptance Officer.
