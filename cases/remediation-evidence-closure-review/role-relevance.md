# Role Relevance

This optional enhancement provides employer-agnostic navigation. It does not establish universal ownership, current hiring demand, professional authorization, or approval authority.

| Role family | Relationship | Basis | Case boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technology Risk | performs | evidence-observed | May perform documentary evidence review; final closure remains separate |
| Control Assurance | performs | evidence-observed | May assess evidence against criteria without accepting risk |
| Quality Assurance | reviews | evidence-observed | May review completeness and traceability, not necessarily own closure |
| Issue and Remediation Ownership | contributes | evidence-observed | Supplies evidence and explanations; does not self-approve closure |
| Internal Audit | reviews | workflow-inferred | May review or consume follow-up work in some operating models |
| Compliance Testing | performs | workflow-inferred | May compare evidence with authorized criteria in some operating models |
| Operational Risk | consumes | workflow-inferred | May consume the recommendation or escalate unresolved risk |
| GRC Operations | contributes | workflow-inferred | May maintain records and coordinate the package |
| Issue Closure Committee | authorizes/owns | case-designed | Fictional decision owner for this case only |
| Risk Acceptance Officer | authorizes/owns | case-designed | Fictional residual-risk authority for this case only |

## Authority note

Technical capability or participation does not create approval, exception, risk-acceptance, legal, sign-off, closure, or production-change authority.
